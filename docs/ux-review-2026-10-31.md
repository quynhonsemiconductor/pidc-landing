## What this PR changes

- `src/styles/global.css` — AIDC hero: two columns at ≥64em, rack figure in its own column;
  band padding 48 px at every width (Q1).
- `src/styles/components.css` — contact page: section heading column at ≥57.5em; the
  three-audience list (Q4); `text-wrap: balance` on hero/section headings.
- `scripts/shots.mjs` — the review set is generated from data (5 pages × 2 locales × 390/768/1440)
  instead of a hand-kept list that had drifted (missing 768, one-off 1024 entries).

No copy, tokens, colours, fonts or routes changed.
