# Competitive landscape — DC / AI-infra marketing homepages
Evidence: 5 screenshots in `../refs/` (1440x900 viewport captures, read 2026-10-29). Hex values are approximations from image analysis.

## DNA cards

### Vantage Data Centers — category standard (wholesale)
- Palette: deep navy/teal-black `#0E1F26–#12262E` full-bleed hero + nav; white headlines; soft grey body; minimal accent (thin rules, small labels).
- Type: all-sans (neutral grotesk); very large light-weight white headline over photo; small caps-ish kicker labels above headline.
- Layout: full-bleed photographic hero, headline left; stats/mega-campus numbers section; classic enterprise section rhythm.
- Imagery: large aerial/architectural campus photography, cinematic dark grade.
- Signature move: dark cinematic photo hero + big capacity numbers (MW, campuses) — the wholesale template.
- Signals: scale, institutional capital, "we build big". Safe, banker-friendly.

### Stack Infrastructure — VOID (cautionary)
- Screenshot is a 123 Reg domain-parking page; live domain confirmed parked 2026-10-29 (redirects to `/lander`); Wayback rate-limited on salvage.
- Not usable as a design reference. Meta-finding: a funded challenger whose web presence lapsed = credibility loss. Site uptime/ownership is part of the trust signal PIDC must never fumble.

### Equinix — incumbent leader
- Palette: white `#FFFFFF` primary; warm light grey `#F7F7F5` alt bands/cards; near-black text; Equinix red `#DA291C` accents/CTAs.
- Type: all-sans (workhorse grotesk); mid-size headlines, heavy link and nav density.
- Layout: portal-like — dense nav, stacked promo cards, many competing CTAs; more IA than narrative.
- Imagery: stock-gloss people/facility photography; some maps/globe motifs for interconnection.
- Signature move: none distinctive — breadth ("platform") shown by listing everything.
- Signals: incumbency and sprawl, not craft. Reads dated; weakest of the captured set.

### CoreWeave — AI compute challenger
- Palette: light mode `#FFFFFF` + pale blue wash `#F2F7FB→#E8F2FA` hero tint; dark navy text; precise graphic pattern work on the right of hero.
- Type: bold sans display at very large sizes; tight modern grotesk; clean mono-ish spec labels.
- Layout: airy single-message sections; hero = big claim + pattern/graphic panel instead of stock photo.
- Imagery: graphic/technical patterns, hardware-adjacent visuals, data-forward panels — least stock-photo dependent.
- Signature move: typography and engineered graphic pattern as the imagery; numbers stated as product specs.
- Signals: engineered, modern, cloud-native confidence. Strongest system discipline in the set.

### Google data centers — leader narrative
- Palette: white + `#F8F9FA` cards; `#202124` text; Google blue `#1A73E8` links/CTAs; green `#188038` headline accent for sustainability; brand colors only in illustration.
- Type: Google Sans — friendly geometric sans, huge comfortable headlines, generous line-height; zero serif/mono.
- Layout: calm editorial narrative scroll; alternating text/photo sections; stat callouts inline.
- Imagery: warm people/community + facility photography; light illustration; a few maps/stats graphics.
- Signature move: narrative arc ("data centers benefit everyone") with plain-language storytelling and big readable numbers.
- Signals: transparency, friendliness, soft power. Craft is high; engineering depth is deliberately shallow.

## Positioning map (x: corporate-polished → engineering-raw; y: photography-led → diagram/data-led)

```
diagram/data-led
      |
      |  CoreWeave (polished, graphic-led)
      |            [ PIDC WHITESPACE ]  <-- engineering-raw + diagram-led: EMPTY
      +---------------------------
      |  Google (polished narrative, mixed photo/illustration)
      |  Equinix (polished, stock photo + link sprawl)
      |  Vantage (polished, photo-led)
photography-led
```
Nobody in the set occupies engineering-raw + diagram-led. Vantage/Equinix/Google all lean on real-building photography — an asset PIDC does not have (pre-construction), so competing there means faking it. CoreWeave proves graphic/typographic imagery can carry an infra brand.

## Category keep-list (user expectations — retain)
- Stats band with hard numbers: MW capacity, sites/markets, PUE — investors scan for these first.
- Dark, serious hero (weight of capital) — even if PIDC's hero is schematic, keep the gravity.
- Footprint/geo proof (map or site plan) — expectation of "where, how big".
- Plain-English benefit line under the technical claim (Google does this best).
- Contact/partner CTA above the fold; nav with clear audience split.

## Where everyone looks the same (PIDC whitespace)
- Interchangeable navy/dark-photo hero formula (Vantage ≈ every wholesale DC).
- Numbers presented as marketing copy, never sourced — no as-of dates, no methodology, no unit discipline.
- Sustainability = green section with vague pledges (Google's is the least vague, still soft).
- Stock-gloss people-in-hard-hats photos; renders passed off as buildings.
- Zero bilingual competence in the set; zero engineering-drawing visual language anywhere.

## Strongest vs weakest
- Strongest: CoreWeave — one clear system (type + engineered graphics), every section obeys it; Google strongest at narrative + readability, weakest on engineering substance.
- Weakest: Equinix — no signature move, link-density over story, stock gloss; looks like a portal, not a position.
- Gap the strongest exposes: commitment to a single visual system and numbers-as-specs; the weakest shows what density + stock photography does to trust.
