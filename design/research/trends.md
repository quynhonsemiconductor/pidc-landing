# Trends — B2B infrastructure / energy marketing sites (2026-10)
Seen-at honesty: "Vantage/CoreWeave/Equinix/Google" = this screenshot set; "trend blogs" = [krishaweb](https://www.krishaweb.com/blog/ux-design-trends), [aufaitux](https://www.aufaitux.com/blog/web-design-trends-2026), [e9digital](https://e9digital.com/6-of-the-best-web-design-trends-in-2021); "public example" = general web knowledge, not from this set. Stack excluded (domain parked).

1. **Dark-first site design** — mainstream (Vantage hero; trend blogs). **Adapt**: one dark schematic hero band for capital gravity, light reading surface elsewhere — full-dark sites now read SaaS-generic, and VI long-form reads worse on dark.
2. **Cyber-neon glow on dark** — rising (trend blogs; NOT in this DC set — telling). **Avoid**: gamer/AI-slop adjacency; capital partners allocating 9-figure checks read glow as decoration, not engineering.
3. **Stats band with hard numbers** — mainstream, category table-stakes (Vantage MW figures, CoreWeave specs, Google inline stats). **Use** — but exceed the set: every PIDC number carries a source + as-of date (design basis, not marketing claim). That alone out-credibles incumbents.
4. **Typographic hero (type-as-image)** — rising (CoreWeave's giant grotesk claims; Stripe is the public example). **Use**: PIDC has no buildings to photograph; a big number + one-line engineering claim is the honest hero.
5. **Engineering drawing / blueprint as brand imagery** — emerging; EMPTY in this category (nobody; closest is CoreWeave's pattern work). **Use as core differentiator**: site plans, single-line diagrams, section cuts — matches pre-construction reality and the anti stock-gloss brief.
6. **Mono technical labels / spec-sheet layer** — rising (CoreWeave spec labels; public example: Stripe docs). **Use**: mono for units, coordinates, drawing annotations. VN caveat: IBM Plex Mono and Roboto Mono have Vietnamese subsets; most boutique monos do not — verify before recommending.
7. **Interactive maps / footprint visuals** — mainstream (Equinix interconnection maps, Google maps). **Adapt**: PIDC has one campus, not a network — a schematic Gia Lai site plan with grid/water/fiber connections beats a lonely map pin.
8. **3D renders / virtual tours** — rising in DC category (public example:Digital Realty tours). **Avoid**: pre-construction renders to capital partners = overpromise risk; construction-doc drawings age honestly, renders age as fiction.
9. **Scroll-driven narrative sections** — rising (Google's narrative scroll). **Adapt lightly**: scroll builds the one-line diagram stage by stage; no parallax gloss, respects "calm, factual" tone.
10. **Bilingual toggle EN/VI as standard** — mainstream expectation, absent in this entire set. **Use** with equal-grade VI (not a sub-domain afterthought); this is a differentiator in Vietnam B2B and forces VN-subset fonts from day one.

## Recombination candidates

**A. "Drawing Set"** *(taste anchor: Stripe-type-led + engineering-drawing tradition)*
- One-line: the site reads like a well-set drawing sheet set — typographic hierarchy of Stripe, annotation language of construction documents.
- Palette: paper `#FAFAF7` ground; near-black ink `#1A1D1F`; one accent = signal red `#C8401A` (callouts, live values) used only where a drawing uses red; hairline `#D8D8D2` rules and grid.
- Type: Space Grotesk or Inter display (both VN subsets) + IBM Plex Mono for annotations/titles-blocks (VN subset). Take: Stripe's scale rhythm + calm; drawing conventions (title block, sheet numbers, revision dates). Avoid: neon, gradients, glass, any photo that isn't documentation. Flag: if tempted toward GT America/Neue Haas class grotesques — they typically lack Vietnamese subsets; Söhne has one but verify at license time.

**B. "Substation Ledger"** *(best pure business fit)*
- One-line: investor-grade spec-sheet site — every section a sourced, dated engineering fact with a footnote, light-mode printable like a bond prospectus.
- Palette: white/`#F7F8F7` ground; `#202124` text; blueprint blue `#1A4E8A` for structure/diagram lines; green `#188038` reserved strictly for sustainability metrics (Google's discipline).
- Type: Inter (VN) body + Beausite-class or Archivo display (Archivo has VN subset) + IBM Plex Mono data. Take: Google's plain-language readability + CoreWeave's spec framing. Avoid: Equinix link-density; hero claims without a source line.

**C. "Control Room" (challenger)** — dark single-band counterpoint
- One-line: mostly-light site with one dark telemetry band (live-ish campus numbers, mono readouts) so the dark mode means something.
- Palette: `#0E1F26` band (Vantage's navy, repurposed for data not cinema); `#7FD1A8` muted phosphor green text; white site elsewhere. Take: Vantage gravity, applied to numbers. Avoid: glow/neon on the dark band (trend 2), or the whole site going dark.

**D. "Terrain & Ledger" (challenger)** — Vietnamese-first editorial
- One-line: bilingual editorial grid where VI and EN are equal citizens and the Gia Lai terrain/site plan is the recurring graphic protagonist.
- Palette: warm paper `#F8F6F1`; ink `#22201C`; earth accent `#B0672B` (laterite soil) for annotations; `#3E6B4F` for environmental metrics.
- Type: Be Vietnam Pro display (designed for VN diacritics — cleanest VI rendering) + Inter or Archivo EN pairing + IBM Plex Mono. Take: Google's narrative arc, told through site/terrain diagrams. Avoid: Google's stock warmth; keep every image a drawing, chart, or sourced map.
